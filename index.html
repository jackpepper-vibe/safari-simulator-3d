A living ecosystem, in three dimensions

Safari Simulator 3D

Stock a reserve with predators and grazers, then keep them in balance through drought, nightfall and the hunt — and walk the camera round them while you do it.

You have ten days as warden, forty grazers and seven predators to bring in — no more. Keep them fed, hold a working food chain, move the surplus on to other reserves and keep the poachers off it.

Populate

Pick a species from the dock, then click the ground to place it. Grazers and predators have separate allowances for the whole tenure, so every animal is a choice you cannot take back.

Sustain

Grazers eat the grass itself and it grows back slowly. Stock more than the pasture can feed and they starve, which is the one thing the brief will not forgive. Watch for drought and fire.

Intervene

Arm the ranger and click an animal to sedate it; the loader and lorry take it out through the gate. Drive at poachers to see them off.
